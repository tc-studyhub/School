Listening, recording and learning tips

Listen once, repeat, then cover the pronunciation and try again. Practise one small lesson at a time. Return tomorrow for a fresh check.

Arabic device voices depend on the browser and installed language voices. If none is available, try Safari or Chrome with an Arabic voice installed. For an authentic Lebanese greeting, use the native reference below. Device speech may be processed by the device’s voice provider.

Record your voice, listen back and compare. Recordings are held only in this page’s memory and disappear when you leave or refresh. They are not uploaded. Recording needs microphone permission and a supported browser. No automatic pronunciation score is given.
