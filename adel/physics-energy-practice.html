Equation sheet · available in every quiz

Equations are provided in your assessment. Practise choosing, rearranging and applying them; show working on paper.

Work done W = F × d
Power / energy P = E ÷ t; E = P × t
Kinetic energy Eₖ = ½ × m × v²
Gravitational potential energy Eₚ = m × g × h
Efficiency useful output ÷ total input; × 100 for %
Energy in kWh power (kW) × time (h), from the booklet definition
Use SI units for joule calculations. Values of g are supplied. This reference is based on booklet pages 2–3 and 8; it is not the teacher’s actual handout.
